polibias
========

Political bias scoring of news articles using local LLMs.

**polibias** uses `Ollama <https://ollama.com>`_ to run multiple small language
models against a corpus of news articles and score them for political bias along
four dimensions: *subject*, *framing*, *treatment*, and *guests*.
